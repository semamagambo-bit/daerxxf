WHO WE ARE

Teens Driving Change

Farm Teens Uganda aims to address youth unemployment and climate change in Uganda Particularly in rural areas where agriculture is a primary source of livelihood by equipping young people with agricultural skills and entrepreneurship opportunities.

Farm Teens Uganda offers comprehensive training programs, mentorship, and support we promote climate-smart agriculture practices and foster innovation, Collaboration among youth and livelihoods to create a more sustainable and inclusive agricultural sectors.

OUR STORY

From Vision to Reality

Farm Teens Uganda was born from a simple yet powerful realization: Uganda's agricultural sector needed a revolution, and young people were the perfect catalysts for change. With 75% of our population under 30, we saw an incredible opportunity to harness youthful energy and innovation.

What started as a small pilot program in Kampala has grown into a nationwide movement, touching lives across 12 districts and training over 250 young agripreneurs. From school gardens to commercial farms, we're proving that agriculture can be both profitable and sustainable.
